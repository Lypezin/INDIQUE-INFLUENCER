# Data Crazy surface brief

Approved visual direction: `Rotas em pauta` for the complete portal, chosen on 2026-10-13. Concept reference: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/rotas-em-pauta.png`. The concept image contains illustrative data and controls; real product functions and data remain authoritative.

## Product and brand

- Keep the Indique e Ganhe name, blue paper-plane logo, and existing blue brand palette.
- Use the light operational layout by default while honoring an explicitly saved light or dark preference.
- Keep the admin desktop and influencer mobile views readable with the same typography, spacing, border, and status-color rules.

## Data Crazy page

- Keep the timeline as the primary content; place the sync control and real source summaries above it.
- Use real Data Crazy and Performance totals, review counts, import timestamps, activity messages, and actor names only.
- Match the Data Crazy courier UUID to the Performance UUID. Do not label unrelated counts as a coverage rate.
- The timeline may show only import/sync events that the panel actually records. Do not synthesize CRM edits or sample names, dates, or counts from the approved comp.
- Keep the full import log one action away and preserve loading, empty, failure, retry, and running states.
- Keep full-collection replacement behind the existing confirmation dialog.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Keep filters and search keyboard accessible with visible focus states and pressed-state semantics.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences; use motion only to clarify an active synchronization state.
- Keep the layout responsive: source cards stack on narrow screens and the cross-match explanation follows the timeline.
